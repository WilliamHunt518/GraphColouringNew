Scenario calibration

The two scenarios differ in mission size and arrival rate, so they offer different amounts of reward. Comparing raw scores would just measure that. These measures normalise by the reward that actually arrived in each session, and are compared within participant — each person played both, so the paired difference removes individual skill.

Verdict

1 of 5 normalised measures differ beyond the paired 95% CI (n=24): Penalty burden. The remaining 4 are indistinguishable.

Why: a second of delay costs 1.24× more per point at stake in Tactical Heavy. Its missions carry a mean penalty rate of 0.208 pts/s against 133 points of reward (1.61 per 1000 points per second), versus 0.099 pts/s against 83 points in Strategic Heavy (1.30). CATEGORY_PENALTY_RATE rises faster across categories than mission reward does, so the larger missions that define Tactical Heavy are charged more for the same elapsed time. Equalising the exchange rate would need the heavier categories' rates scaled by about 0.80.
